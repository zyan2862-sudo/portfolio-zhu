# Replace these image placeholders

Add your own, licensed photographs using these exact filenames:

- `hero-winter-event.jpg` — winter event venue or ice/snow event
- `hero-ski-resort.jpg` — Japanese ski resort or mountain
- `hero-event-operations.jpg` — sport event operations
- `beijing-project.jpg` — your Beijing 2022 project photo
- `ski-research.jpg` — your ski resort fieldwork
- `yuru-sports.jpg` — your Yuru Sports operations photo
- `community-sports.jpg` — community sport or youth sumo event
- `profile.jpg` — professional portrait

Recommended: JPG or WebP, at least 1600 px wide for hero/research images and
800 px wide for cards. The site shows designed fallback panels if files are
missing.
